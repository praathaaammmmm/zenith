# Case Study: Dropbox MVP & Validation Strategy

How Drew Houston validated cloud file synchronization before writing a full production backend.

## The Strategy:
Instead of spending 6 months building complex multi-operating system sync drivers, Drew created a 3-minute video demonstration showing how seamlessly files synced across folders.

## The Outcome:
- The video was posted to Hacker News and Digg.
- The beta waiting list expanded overnight from 5,000 to 75,000 prospective users.
- Validated massive market demand and user willingness to install a desktop sync utility.

## Key Takeaway for Founders:
Demonstrate the core outcome and magic moment before over-engineering backend infrastructure.
