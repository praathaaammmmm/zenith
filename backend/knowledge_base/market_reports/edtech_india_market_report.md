# EdTech & AI Tutor Market Analysis (India & Emerging Markets)

Overview of AI tutoring, personalized learning, and test prep technology adoption.

## Market Metrics:
- India EdTech & Test Prep Market Size: Estimated $10.4B by 2028, with AI tutoring growing at 32% CAGR.
- Target Demographic: K-12 students, competitive exam aspirants (JEE, NEET, UPSC), and tier 2/3 college students seeking affordable 1-on-1 guidance.

## Key Pain Points:
1. High cost of private human coaching institutes ($1,500 - $3,000/year).
2. Lack of instant doubt resolution during late-night self-study.
3. Vernacular language barriers in traditional standard English textbooks.

## Winning Business Model:
- Freemium micro-subscriptions (e.g., ₹299 - ₹799/month) or per-doubt resolution credits.
- WhatsApp & mobile-first integration for low latency access.
