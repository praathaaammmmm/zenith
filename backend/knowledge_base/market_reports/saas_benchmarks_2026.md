# 2026 B2B SaaS & AI Market Benchmarks

Global B2B SaaS and AI tooling market metrics and benchmarks for early-stage startups.

## Pricing Tiers:
- **Solo/Pro Tier**: $29 - $49/month per seat. High self-serve conversion, target ACV $350 - $600.
- **Team Growth Tier**: $199 - $349/month (up to 10 seats). High retention, payback period < 6 months.
- **Agency / Enterprise Tier**: $499 - $1,200/month. Includes custom API limits, white-label exports, and priority support.

## Key Performance Indicators:
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR): 110%+ target for B2B SaaS.
- CAC Payback Period: Under 12 months for SMB/Mid-Market.
- Trial to Paid Conversion Rate: 8% - 15% for product-led growth (PLG) self-serve tools.
