# Customer Development Framework (Steve Blank & Rob Fitzpatrick)

The Four Steps to the Epiphany framework for early-stage startup validation.

## 1. Customer Discovery
- Test founder vision against real customer pain points.
- Identify early adopters: users who actively search for a solution, have budget allocated, and have tried solving the problem manually.

## 2. Customer Validation
- Build a repeatable sales and onboarding workflow.
- Test whether early adopters are willing to exchange money or committed pilot agreements for the MVP.

## 3. Customer Creation
- Drive end-user demand and channel growth after confirming initial Product-Market Fit (PMF).

## 4. Company Building
- Transition from an informal learning organization into formal functional departments.

## The Mom Test Rules:
1. Talk about their life instead of your idea.
2. Ask about specifics in the past instead of generics or opinions about the future.
3. Talk less and listen more.
