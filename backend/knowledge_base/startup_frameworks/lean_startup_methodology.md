# Lean Startup Methodology & Build-Measure-Learn Loop

The core objective of the Lean Startup approach is to eliminate waste and test hypotheses as early as possible.

## Key Principles:
1. **Validated Learning**: Founders must test core value and growth hypotheses through empirical experiments rather than vanity metrics.
2. **Build-Measure-Learn**: Turn startup ideas into Minimum Viable Products (MVPs), measure real customer reactions, and learn whether to pivot or persevere.
3. **Innovation Accounting**: Track actionable metrics (e.g., active user retention, willingness-to-pay conversion) rather than vanity metrics (e.g., total signups).

## Customer Discovery Rules:
- Never ask customers if they *would* buy a hypothetical future feature. Ask what solutions they currently pay for and where friction exists today.
- Look for non-consumption: users who have hacked together spreadsheets, manual scripts, or multiple apps to solve a painful problem.
